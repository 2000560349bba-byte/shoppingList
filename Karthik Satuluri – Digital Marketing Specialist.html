How I work

Creative testing on Meta

I test creatives, headlines and offers against relevant audiences, track click-through rate, cost per click and conversions, then move budget toward the ads that perform best.

Turning around struggling accounts

I start by finding what isn’t working: targeting, ad performance, tracking or landing pages. Then I test fixes and tighten spend to get more conversions at a lower cost. At Thryv this meant restructuring inherited accounts, which produced the 22% cost-per-lead drop.
